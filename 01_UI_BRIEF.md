# UI Brief — Research Portfolio V2

## Product thesis
This portfolio is an evidence interface for an unconventional independent researcher. Its job is to compress a large body of work into a credible, legible public surface for researchers, grant reviewers, technical referees, and investors.

## Audience order
1. technical research / grant reviewer
2. high-end AI researcher or potential collaborator
3. technically sophisticated investor/funder
4. general visitor

## Desired emotional arc
`Who is this?` → `This is unusually coherent.` → `There is real experimental discipline here.` → `The negative results make the positive claims more credible.` → `There is a larger private program behind these public branches.`

## Brand traits
Precise. Independent. Experimental. Economically grounded. Falsifiable. Sparse. Serious.

Avoid: prodigy branding, self-congratulation, hype, 'revolutionary', 'AGI breakthrough', giant percentage counters with no context.

## Homepage hero
Prefer a two-column or strong editorial layout rather than a centered startup hero.

Primary line:
Saurav Sharma
Independent Researcher & Technical Builder

Research thesis:
Decision systems under hidden mechanisms, consequential information, binding constraints, and rare failures.

Background metadata should be secondary:
- Independent Researcher
- BS student, IIT Madras
- Markets / trading background

## Evidence design
Results should be presented as compact evidence blocks with context labels, e.g.
- `15/15 exact — frozen diagnostic`
- `14/20 failures — omitted working-capital mechanism`
- `5,000-world fresh holdout — exact enumeration`

Never present a naked metric without the experiment label.

## Research visual language
Use diagrams only if they clarify causal structure or research progression. Prefer simple lines, grids, boundary diagrams, state/action notation, and small data marks over decorative illustration.

## About page
Should make the person legible without turning into a conventional resume. The hierarchy is:
research trajectory → markets intuition → IIT Madras education → current direction.

## Mobile
On mobile, preserve hierarchy and evidence context. Do not collapse result labels into meaningless number tiles.
