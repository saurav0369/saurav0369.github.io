01 — Research thesis

Capability is not reliable action.

High predictive or reasoning capability does not by itself guarantee reliable action when the decision depends on identifying the governing rule, establishing whether available evidence supports that rule in the region the plan will visit, stress-testing rare regimes, or preserving future options while acquiring information.

  1. evidence
  2. governing structure
  3. support
  4. stress
  5. planning
  6. verified execution

The public research boundary can be summarized as: evidence → governing structure → support → stress → planning → verified execution

03 — What the public work is for

Standalone research probes

REC, microstructure, and private credit are not simply marketing examples for AEI. They are standalone research probes of different pieces of the broader problem: timing information acquisition, valuing an inquiry as an intervention, and identifying when model error moves a reached feasibility boundary.

05 · What remains unproven

The decisive next step.

Boundary

The existing evidence does not establish broad general intelligence, universal superiority over frontier models, or production reliability in open-world finance. The next decisive step is externally specified and independently auditable validation across new world families, model providers, and increasingly realistic economic structure.

Disclosure policy. Public materials should establish the problem, evidence quality, and research trajectory without becoming a reconstruction manual for the private architecture.
