Decision systems for worlds where the rules are not fully visible.

I study reliable action under hidden mechanisms, consequential information acquisition, binding constraints, and rare failures—especially in economic and financial environments.

My work combines controlled experiments, exact planning where possible, adversarial baselines, frozen evaluations, and explicit negative results. A broader private program, Autonomous Economic Intelligence (AEI), connects these lines.

Methods

How I work.

M.1

Mechanism isolation

Small controlled environments are used to separate a proposed failure mode from generic model weakness.

M.2

Prospective testing

When feasible, worlds, seed rules, criteria, and analysis are frozen before confirmatory evaluation.

M.3

Adversarial self-review

Oracle contamination, generic-planner attacks, non-identifiability, failed controls, and null results are retained.
