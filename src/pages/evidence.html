02 — Research standards

How claims are handled.

S.1

Freeze before confirmatory evaluation

Where a result is treated as prospective evidence, the environment, seed rule, analysis, or evaluation criteria are fixed before the corresponding holdout is run.

S.2

Keep negative evidence

Generic-planner successes, oracle contamination, non-identifiability, failed controls, and null results are retained rather than silently dropped.

S.3

Separate discovery from confirmation

Development worlds and post-hoc follow-ups are not relabeled as prospective confirmation.

S.4

State external-validity limits

Synthetic mechanism tests are not presented as prevalence estimates for deployed systems.
