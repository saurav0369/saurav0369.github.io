01 · Research identity

From economic agents to a research program.

My current program began from attempts to build economic decision agents and evolved into a broader investigation of where capable systems fail when they must identify governing structure before acting, acquire information without destroying future options, or respect constraints that become decisive only under stress.

I work independently and use controlled environments, exact solvers where possible, frozen evaluations, adversarial baselines, and explicit negative results to separate mechanism claims from benchmark narratives.
