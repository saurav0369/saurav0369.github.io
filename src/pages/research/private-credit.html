01 · Question

A representation-to-decision failure.

Private-credit decision systems may optimize against encoded liquidity and contractual constraints. This study isolates a representation-to-decision failure: an optimizer can be exact for its encoded model and still select an infeasible action when an omitted stress mechanism changes a reached constraint.

03 · Experimental design

Original prospective holdout.

After development, the environment, deterministic seed rule, analysis, primary criteria, and bootstrap procedure were fixed before evaluating the fresh holdout. A designed line-cut control removed a revolver that the selected nominal recession path did not use. The boundary-moving treatment instead introduced an omitted working-capital drain.

The designed line-cut control.

05 · Separate balanced follow-up

Two constraint families.

Because the original 20-world holdout contained 19 minimum-cash worlds and only one leverage-style world, a separate source-anchored follow-up was frozen before its intervention outcomes were computed. It tests 20 fresh worlds per constraint family across a pre-specified 5/10/15/20-unit shock grid.

At the 15-unit shock, failures are 14/20 for minimum-cash worlds and 17/20 for leverage-style worlds, while the exact stressed optimum remains feasible in all 40.

06 · What the evidence covers

Scope of the evidence.

Frozen 20-world holdout; line-cut control; 14/20 failures under omitted working-capital mechanism; stressed optimum feasible 20/20.

Integrity boundary

The follow-up is not represented as hash-continuous confirmation of the original v1 archive because the exact original archive was unavailable for byte-for-byte comparison. It is reported separately as robustness evidence.

07 · What it does not establish

Not a prevalence estimate.

Boundary

The environments are synthetic near-boundary mechanism tests. They do not estimate prevalence in deployed private-credit systems and omit many institutional features such as amendments, cure rights, lender discretion, sponsor support, and renegotiation.
