# Claims and disclosure policy

## Blind review
- Do not publicly write `AISTATS 2027 under review` for REC while blind review is active.
- Do not use ICAIF workshop names as public prestige signals before anonymity concerns are resolved.
- Use `Research manuscript, 2026` on public pages.

## IIT Madras
- IIT Madras may be listed under **Education** because Saurav is a BS student.
- Research affiliation remains **Independent Researcher** for independently conducted work.
- Do not imply IIT Madras sponsorship, supervision, or endorsement unless specifically documented.

## Trading background
- Public wording should say `hands-on experience studying and trading financial markets` or equivalent.
- Do not imply institutional trading employment, professional AUM, audited P&L, or years of professional experience unless separately documented.

## AEI
Public: problem framing, high-level research thesis, selected internally labeled evidence, evidence limitations.
Private: exact identification machinery, probing/acquisition policies, architecture-selection details, successor design, reconstruction-grade source and full evaluation suite.

## Evidence language
- `internally reproducible` is not `independently reproduced`.
- `manuscript` is not `publication`.
- synthetic mechanism tests are not prevalence estimates.
- retained negative results should be visible.
